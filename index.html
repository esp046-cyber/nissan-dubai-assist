Driving in the UAE

Highways and Salik

On Sheikh Zayed Rd (E11) and Emirates Rd (E611) keep right unless overtaking. Radars in Dubai typically tolerate about 20 km/h over the limit; Abu Dhabi has no buffer. Salik gates charge automatically, so keep your tag balance topped up. In coastal morning fog use low beams and double your following distance.

50C summers and sandstorms

Hot asphalt raises tyre pressure and blowout risk, so check cold. In dust, switch AC to recirculate and slow down. Hazard lights while moving are only for an immediate hazard. Watch the temperature gauge; pull over safely if it climbs.

Dunes: Al Qudra, Lahbab, Liwa

Drop tyres to 15-18 PSI in soft sand, select 4H (4L for steep climbs), lock the rear diff where fitted, and switch off VDC/traction control in deep sand. Crest dunes at an angle, never straight. Re-inflate with a compressor and wash the undercarriage afterward.

    Damage prevention

    Never overfill dual-tank Patrols

    Stop fuelling at the first click of the pump nozzle. Extra fuel floods the EVAP canister (14950-1LA0B), mixes with dust and can trigger the sub-tank transfer lockout.

    Replace the firewall heater T-pipe

    The plastic heater connector (14053-1LA1A) goes brittle in UAE heat. Replace it every 80,000 km or fit an aluminium fitting before it snaps and dumps the coolant.

    CVT thermal survival

    Avoid holding high revs in manual mode on Jebel Jais or Jebel Hafeet in summer. Change NS-3 fluid and the external cartridge filter (31726-28X0A) every 40,000 km.

    Protect the HBMC suspension

    Take Dubai speed bumps under 20 km/h and never aim a pressure washer or harsh degreaser at the HBMC accumulator valves.

    After the dunes

    Idle 5 minutes with AC off and bonnet up before shutdown. Re-inflate to 35 PSI before going above 60 km/h on tarmac. Wash packed sand out of the wheel barrels, which causes vibration at 100-120 km/h.

    Storms and wadi floods

    Never enter standing water deeper than 500 mm in a Patrol (factory maximum wading depth is 700 mm; the margin covers bow waves from oncoming vehicles) or 250-300 mm in a sedan or 300-350 mm in the X-Trail. If unavoidable, hold a steady 5-8 km/h, never stop mid-water, then check the differential breathers (38356-1LA0A) and dry the brakes.
