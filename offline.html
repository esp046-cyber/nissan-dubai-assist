Engine overheating

  1. Switch AC off, heater on full.
  2. Pull over safely, engine off, bonnet open.
  3. Never open the radiator cap while hot. Wait 30+ minutes.

Stranded in the desert (Al Qudra, Liwa)

  1. Stay with the vehicle; shade under it is cooler.
  2. Ration water, move only at dawn or dusk if needed.
  3. Share your location by SMS; call 999.
