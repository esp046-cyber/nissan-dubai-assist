# UAE and Dubai driving rules (summary)
- Dubai radars typically tolerate about 20 km/h over the posted limit; Abu Dhabi enforces without buffer. Rules and fines change: verify with RTA (8009090).
- Hazard lights while moving are only for an immediate hazard.
- Salik: tag balance must cover each gate crossing.
- Keep a reflective triangle and fire extinguisher in the car.
